# Module 51: Research Engineering

Reading, reproducing, and extending AI research — the skills between
"uses models" and "improves models".

## What You Will Learn

- Reading a paper: structure, claims, evidence, limitations
- Literature review and finding research gaps
- Reproducibility: baselines, ablations, statistical rigor
- Experiment design: hypotheses, controls, metrics
- Implementing papers from scratch
- Benchmarking honestly (same splits, seeds, compute)
- Writing: technical reports, results, and limitations sections
- Presenting and defending work
- Building a research log / experiment notebook practice

## Prerequisites

- 16_ML_FROM_SCRATCH, 19_DEEP_LEARNING (implementing skills)

## Exit Criteria

- [ ] You reproduced a published result and documented the gaps
- [ ] You designed an ablation study with a clear hypothesis
- [ ] You wrote a 2-page technical report on your experiment
