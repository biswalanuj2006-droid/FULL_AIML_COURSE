================================================================================
PRACTICE - 59 RECOMMENDER SYSTEMS
================================================================================

PART A - CONCEPTUAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Explicit vs implicit feedback: for each signal (star rating, view,
   purchase, skip, add-to-cart), say which kind it is and one way the
   missingness is ambiguous.
2. Why must a matrix-factorization model beat the popularity baseline
   before you trust any offline gain? What does the baseline capture
   that the metric does not?
3. Explain latent factor k in words: what does it mean that user u and
   item i are close in factor space? Why is k a bias-variance knob?
4. Two-stage retrieval: why can you not score 80M items per user with a
   deep ranking model? Where does ANN search enter?

PART B - NUMERICAL (do by hand)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Ratings matrix (users U1..U3 x items I1..I3):
        U1: I1=5, I2=3
        U2: I1=4, I3=2
        U3: I2=1, I3=4
   Initialize p_u = q_i = (1,1) for all, lr = 0.05, lambda = 0.1.
   Perform TWO Funk-SGD updates on the pair (U1, I1). Show every step
   and the updated predictions.
6. ALS closed form: with Q fixed, write p_u = (Q_u^T Q_u + lambda I)^-1
   Q_u^T r_u. Explain each term for a user with 2 ratings and k=3.
7. NDCG@2: recommender A returns [relevant, relevant], B returns
   [relevant, irrelevant]. Relevance = 1/0. Compute DCG@2 and NDCG@2
   for both. Why does B's score punish the second position?

PART C - CODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. Implement Funk-SGD matrix factorization in numpy on the MovieLens-
   style data you generate (or use code/ or sklearn-free). Track RMSE
   on a time-based holdout; plot RMSE vs k in {2,5,10,20}.
9. Implement the Hu-Koren implicit ALS (section 4 of the lesson) on a
   synthetic click matrix; verify that popular-but-unclicked items get
   lower scores than clicked ones and that confidence alpha matters.
10. Ranking evaluation: given a held-out set of one positive item per
    user, compute HitRate@10 and NDCG@10 for (a) popularity, (b) your
    MF model. State which wins and why.
11. Two-tower: build user and item feature matrices, a small dot-product
    model with in-batch negative sampling trained on synthetic
    interactions; embed all items and serve the top-k by numpy
    nearest neighbor for one user.
12. Feedback-loop simulation: train MF, generate "clicks" from its top
    recommendations only, retrain, repeat 10 rounds; measure how the
    catalog's diversity collapses vs a model trained with 10% random
    exploration.

PART D - DEBUGGING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
13. Offline NDCG +8%, live CTR -15%. Give three candidate causes and
    the A/B or analysis that isolates each.
14. Your ALS model recommends only blockbusters for every user. Name two
    mechanisms (popularity feedback, implicit count bias) and one fix
    each.
15. Users who rated 5 stars dominate the training loss; new-item
    recommendations are random. Diagnose and propose the objective
    change.
16. A feature "item purchased after recommendation" is a column in the
    training table. What is leaking, and what is the correct split?
17. Evaluation says your recommender is excellent but a manual review
    shows terrible diversity. Which metric are you not reporting, and
    what metric would you add (MMR / intra-list diversity)?

PART E - REAL WORLD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
18. Movie streaming, 200M users, 80M items: sketch the two-stage system
    with candidate sources and ranking features; where is cold start
    handled; how would you detect the popularity feedback loop in
    production metrics?
19. Marketplace with seasonal items: items live 6 weeks. Why does pure
    CF fail, and how do you add item metadata + recency?
20. You must defend "recommend the user the product they already bought"
    - not possible, but the model still returns it. Which evaluation
    bug makes this possible (leakage of the label into features)?
21. (Interview) Explain matrix factorization to a PM in 3 sentences;
    then to an engineer with the ALS update. Why two audiences?
22. (Interview) "How would you evaluate a recommender before launch,
    and how would you know your offline eval is lying?" Answer with
    policy bias, position bias, and time split.

PART F - EXPERT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
23. Implement causal / propensity-corrected evaluation (inverse
    propensity weighting on logged click data) and show the correction
    changes your model ranking vs naive logged-data evaluation.
24. Design an exploration policy for ranking that guarantees each item
    a minimum exposure while maximizing expected reward; implement a
    simplified epsilon-greedy version and measure the regret.
25. (Open) Debias a feedback loop analytically: model CTR as
    p(click | item quality, exposure), and derive why naive training
    confounds the two. What data would let you separate them?
