THINK.TXT - 09 CLASSIFICATION
================================================================================

1. Logistic regression is a "linear classifier" yet produces curved
   probability contours under sigmoid. Resolve this apparent contradiction
   precisely (decision boundary vs probability surface).
2. Decision boundaries: for KNN, SVM (linear + RBF), logistic regression,
   and a depth-3 tree on the same 2D dataset, sketch/simulate the
   boundaries. Explain WHY each looks the way it does in terms of its
   objective function.
3. Class imbalance of 99:1. Accuracy says 99%. Derive why precision-recall
   is the honest pair here, and what happens to ROC-AUC when the positive
   class is rare.
4. A tree model and a logistic model both reach 0.85 F1. When would you
   still strongly prefer each? Consider: features, data size, latency,
   regulation, and interactions.
5. Softmax outputs "probabilities" that are systematically overconfident.
   Where does the overconfidence come from and what are the fixes
   (temperature, calibration, OOD)?
6. SVM with RBF kernel has an implicit feature space of infinite
   dimension, yet generalizes. Explain this paradox using margin theory.
7. You have 20,000 rows and 2,000 features (many irrelevant), binary
   target. Compare logistic (with penalty), RBF-SVM, and boosted trees in
   terms of expected performance and why.
8. Threshold choice: moving the decision threshold from 0.5 to 0.9 changes
   precision/recall but not the model. Formalize the trade-off when the
   cost of a false positive is 10x a false negative. What is the optimal
   threshold rule and its assumption?
9. Label noise at 10% flips random training labels. Which classifiers are
   most robust to it and why? Which metric degrades first?
10. Nearest-neighbor classifiers suffer the curse of dimensionality.
    Quantify: at 100 dimensions, what is the expected ratio of
    nearest-to-farthest distance for uniform data, and why does that break
    KNN?
11. Multi-class with 500 classes: contrast one-vs-rest logistic,
    softmax regression, trees, and KNN in training cost and accuracy
    behavior. Which scales?
12. Two models: one predicts 0.9 for all positives, another predicts 0.55
    for all positives. Both are always correct. Which is better and how
    do you measure it (calibration vs discrimination)?
13. Derive why Naive Bayes can be optimal even when its independence
    assumption is badly violated (think: ranking vs density estimation).
14. OPEN PROBLEM: threshold-free classifier evaluation is an active
    research area (AUC, PR-AUC, cost curves). Argue which single metric
    you would standardize on for deployment decisions and defend it
    against its known critics.
