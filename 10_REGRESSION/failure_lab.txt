================================================================================
FAILURE LAB - 10 REGRESSION
Deliberately broken/difficult setups. For each: reproduce, DIAGNOSE with
evidence, fix, and report. Do not guess - run the experiment.
================================================================================

LAB 1 - THE INVISIBLE LEAK
  House-price model with R2 = 0.97 on validation. One planted feature
  contains the answer (e.g., "sale_price_minus_1" style, a future
  transaction date, or the target lagged from a merged file).
  Tasks:
   1. Find the leak via EDA + ablation.
   2. Explain the mechanism and why R2 hid it.
   3. Fix and show the honest R2.

LAB 2 - THE MULTICOLLINEARITY MELTDOWN
  Two features correlate 0.999. OLS coefficients are huge, opposite
  signs, and unstable across CV folds.
  Tasks:
   1. Reproduce the instability (report coefficients + fold-to-fold
      variance).
   2. Diagnose via condition number / VIF.
   3. Compare fixes: drop one, Ridge, Lasso, PCA - report test RMSE and
      coefficient stability for each.

LAB 3 - THE OUTLIER TAKEOVER
  One row is 1000x the range of the others.
  Tasks:
   1. Show the fitted line before and after removing the point (plot).
   2. Distinguish leverage, residual, and influence for this point
      (hat values, Cook's distance).
   3. Compare fixes: robust (Huber) regression vs winsorizing vs
      removing - which is honest when the outlier is REAL data?

LAB 4 - HETEROSCEDASTICITY BLINDNESS
  Residuals grow with the fitted value.
  Tasks:
   1. Detect it (residual vs fitted plot; Goldfeld-Quandt or Breusch-
      Pagan if available).
   2. Explain what it does to your confidence intervals (they are wrong
      - why?).
   3. Fix with a log transform or weighted least squares and show the
      corrected residuals.

LAB 5 - THE TIME-ORDERED TRAP
  A demand dataset sorted by date; someone used random K-fold and got
  RMSE 20% better than walk-forward.
  Tasks:
   1. Reproduce both evaluations and the gap.
   2. Explain precisely why random CV is optimistic here.
   3. Fix with TimeSeriesSplit and report the honest number.

LAB 6 - DEGREE-15 POLYNOMIAL DISASTER
  A degree-15 polynomial interpolates every point (train RMSE ~ 0) but
  explodes between points.
  Tasks:
   1. Reproduce - plot the fitted curve and show the wild swings.
   2. Diagnose: is it bias or variance? Use learning curves.
   3. Fix with Ridge (show that penalty tames the curve) or lower
      degree; report the chosen model and test RMSE.
   4. Explain what the large coefficients were doing numerically
      (Vandermonde conditioning).
