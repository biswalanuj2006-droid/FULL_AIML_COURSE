1. Derive the OLS solution (X^T X)^-1 X^T y. When does X^T X fail to be
   invertible, and what do Ridge/Lasso do about it differently?
2. R-squared = 0.99 yet predictions are useless. Construct a scenario
   (think: extrapolation, heteroscedasticity, outliers, leakage) and
   explain which diagnostic catches it.
3. Multicollinearity: two features correlate 0.99. OLS coefficients
   explode in magnitude and flip sign. Explain why, then compare how
   Ridge, Lasso, and dropping a feature handle it.
4. The Gauss-Markov assumptions: state them, then for each give a real
   dataset where it fails and the consequence.
5. Outliers with high leverage vs high residual: distinguish them and
   explain why influence = leverage x discrepancy. What does each do to
   the fitted line?
6. Lasso drives coefficients exactly to zero; Ridge never does. Explain
   geometrically (constraint regions) and say which you would use for
   feature selection and why.
7. Prediction intervals vs confidence intervals: what does each promise
   and which one would a business actually want? How do you build a
   prediction interval for a tree-based model?
8. You regress house price on area. Residuals grow with area. What
   assumption fails, what transformation fixes it, and what does the
   log-log coefficient now mean?
9. Quantile regression at tau=0.9: what exactly does it estimate and when
   would you choose it over OLS with a 10% trimmed mean?
10. Regularization strength: as lambda -> inf, Ridge coefficients go to 0.
    Derive the bias-variance path and find where test error is minimized
    without looking at the test set (how?).
11. A linear model with polynomial degree 15 interpolates every training
    point yet fails on validation. Show the mechanism (Vandermonde,
    condition number) and the fix.
12. Time-ordered data and a random split give optimistic R2. Construct the
    exact mechanism and the correct evaluation.
13. Comparing models: Model A: MAE 3.1, Model B: MAE 3.0. Is B better?
    Design the experiment that answers this with confidence.
14. OPEN PROBLEM: model selection for regression under heteroscedastic
    noise remains partly heuristic. Propose a criterion that handles
    non-constant variance better than RMSE/R2 and discuss its
    limitations.
