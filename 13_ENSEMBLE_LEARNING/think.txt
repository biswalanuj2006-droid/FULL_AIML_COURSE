================================================================================
THINK.TXT - 13 ENSEMBLE LEARNING
================================================================================

1. Bagging reduces variance: derive why averaging N i.i.d. predictors
   with variance sigma^2 gives sigma^2/N, and explain why trees in a
   forest are NOT independent - what does the correlation do to the
   formula, and how does feature sampling fight it?
2. Boosting reduces bias: a shallow tree fit to residuals improves a
   constant model. Formalize gradient boosting as gradient descent in
   function space and identify the "step" and the "learning rate".
3. Why can boosting overfit when the ensemble of weak learners gets
   strong? Give the mechanism and the three controls that fight it.
4. Random Forest vs Gradient Boosting on: (a) wide sparse data,
   (b) tall clean data, (c) noisy labels. Argue which wins each and why.
5. AdaBoost upweights misclassified samples. Derive the weight update and
   explain why AdaBoost is fragile to label noise while RF is not.
6. Stacking: why does a meta-learner over plain averaging help, and what
   leakage trap appears if the meta-features are generated on the same
   data the base models were trained on? How do you generate honest
   meta-features?
7. Voting: hard vs soft voting. When is soft voting worse than its best
   member (think calibration), and how do you fix it?
8. XGBoost adds a second-order (Hessian) approximation and explicit
   regularization. Explain what each buys over plain gradient boosting
   and why it converges faster.
9. Feature importance disagreement: RF, gradient boosting, and
   permutation importance rank features differently on the same data.
   Explain the mechanisms and which one you trust for a regulatory
   submission.
10. 100 trees vs 10,000 trees: where do the gains saturate and how do you
    detect the point empirically? What does the OOB curve look like as
    you add trees?
11. You have 50M rows. Contrast RF, XGBoost, LightGBM (histogram,
    leaf-wise), and CatBoost (ordered target statistics) on memory,
    training time, and accuracy, and pick for the dataset.
12. An ensemble of an SVM, a logistic model, and a tree beats each alone,
    yet a single deep model beats the ensemble. Construct the scenario
    and explain why.
13. OPEN PROBLEM: there is no settled theory of WHEN to stop adding models
    to an ensemble or how to weight them optimally beyond heuristics
    (stacking, Bayesian model averaging). Propose a criterion and its
    failure modes.
