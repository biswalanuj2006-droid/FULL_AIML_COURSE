THINK.TXT - 20 CNN

1. Convolutions give translation equivariance. Define equivariance
   formally and contrast with invariance - which one does pooling
   provide and what does each cost you?
2. Why do CNNs need far fewer parameters than MLPs on images? Count
   parameters for a 224x224x3 image into a 1000-class MLP vs a ResNet
   conv stack and explain where the saving comes from.
3. Receptive field: a 3x3 conv stacked 10 times sees what fraction of a
   224x224 input? Derive the RF formula and say why dilation exists.
4. Output size math: 32x32 input, 5x5 kernel, padding 2, stride 2.
   Compute the output size and state the general formula. What does
   padding do to border information and parameter sharing?
5. Max pooling loses location information - yet classification works.
   Explain what pooling's invariance buys and where it hurts
   (segmentation, precise localization).
6. ResNet's skip connections were introduced to fight degradation, not
   just vanishing gradients. Construct the argument: why can a deeper
   plain net have HIGHER training error, and how does the identity
   path make depth free?
7. Transfer learning: freeze backbone vs fine-tune all. When is each
   right, and why does a small learning rate on pretrained weights
   matter (feature corruption)?
8. Data augmentation is a prior. Argue that random crops/color jitter
   encode "these transformations do not change the label" - and say
   which augmentations would violate that prior for medical images vs
   satellite images.
9. A CNN gets 99% on training and 60% on validation with a 100-class
   dataset of 500 images/class. Diagnose the gap and rank: dropout,
   augmentation, pretraining, label smoothing, weight decay - with the
   expected effect of each.
10. Feature maps: shallow layers detect edges, deep layers detect
    objects. Why does this hierarchy emerge from gradient descent
    rather than being designed? What does it predict about
    transferability at each depth?
11. Batch size 256 with BatchNorm on CNNs trains faster but worse than
    batch 32 on the same epochs. Explain the BN-batch-size interaction
    and the fix (ghost batch norm).
12. Your CNN is 10x too slow for real-time video. Rank interventions by
    (speedup, accuracy cost): quantization, depthwise convs,
    distillation, pruning, smaller input, ONNX. Justify the top two.
13. OPEN PROBLEM: adversarial examples are imperceptible perturbations
    that flip CNN decisions. Argue why the linearity-in-high-dim
    explanation (Goodfellow 2014) is incomplete and what remains
    unexplained about their transferability.
