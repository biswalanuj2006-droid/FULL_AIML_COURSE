DEBUGGING PRACTICE (10)
1. Mask applied after softmax -> leak. Fix + test.
2. Shape error Q@K instead of Q@K.T.
3. NaN after 100 steps: logits un-scaled, grad explosion, lr spike.
4. Shifted labels off by one: logits[t] vs target[t] mismatch.
5. Tokenizer mismatch train/serve -> garbage output.
6. Trainer resumes but loss jumps: optimizer state not loaded.
7. LoRA not learning: B initialized nonzero, or base not frozen, or
   alpha/r = 0.
8. INT4 model much worse on your task: check scale calibration on YOUR
   data.
9. Streaming endpoint dies mid-token: no error handling in generator.
10. RAG answers hallucinate: retrieval hit-rate 0.9 yet answers wrong -
    instrument context faithfulness (never trust the answer alone).
