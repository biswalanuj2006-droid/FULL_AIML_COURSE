================================================================================
PRACTICE - 61 REINFORCEMENT LEARNING FOUNDATIONS
================================================================================

PART A - CONCEPTUAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MDP components: from a chess game, a robot vacuum, and a news
   recommender, extract S, A, p(s'|s,a), r, and gamma. Which one is
   truly Markovian, and what do the others do about it?
2. Exploration vs exploitation: give one epsilon-greedy and one
   non-epsilon method (optimistic initialization, UCB, Thompson
   sampling) and the setting where each shines.
3. On-policy vs off-policy: define both, and explain why replay buffers
   REQUIRE off-policy learning.
4. Model-free vs model-based: what does "model-free" actually mean, and
   what would a learned model buy you (planning, sample efficiency)?

PART B - MATH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Write V_pi(s) and Q_pi(s,a), the Bellman equations for both, and the
   Bellman OPTIMALITY equation. Mark which terms are expectations over
   the environment vs the policy.
6. Contraction: show the Bellman operator T(V) = max_a [ r + gamma E V ]
   satisfies ||T(V1) - T(V2)||_inf <= gamma ||V1 - V2||_inf and why
   that guarantees iteration converges to V*.
7. TD error: delta_t = r_{t+1} + gamma V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t). Show that
   in expectation the TD update moves V toward the true value (derive
   E[delta | s_t] and connect it to the Bellman residual).
8. Policy gradient theorem: state it, then derive the gradient of
   J(theta) = E[sum gamma^t r] w.r.t. theta using the log-derivative
   trick. Where does grad log pi come from and why is there no
   gradient through the environment?
9. Baseline: prove E[ grad_theta log pi_theta(a|s) * b(s) ] = 0, hence
   the advantage form A(s,a) = Q - V is unbiased with lower variance.

PART C - CODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. Tabular Q-learning on a 5x5 gridworld (implement the environment
    yourself; gymnasium optional): epsilon-greedy with decay, report
    episode returns and the resulting policy. Verify the policy is
    optimal (compare to value iteration).
11. SARSA vs Q-learning on the same grid with a stochastic transition
    (10% random slip): show which learns the safer policy and why
    (on-policy accounts for exploration).
12. DQN with replay + target net on CartPole-style control (gymnasium
    if available, else your gridworld): ablate replay (off) and target
    (sync every step) - show which ablation breaks and how.
13. REINFORCE with a softmax policy on a simple 2-armed bandit with
    known means; plot the policy over time; then add a baseline and
    measure the variance reduction in gradient estimates.
14. Actor-critic: implement a small advantage actor-critic on CartPole
    (or gridworld): critic = MSE regression on returns, actor = policy
    gradient with A_t = return - V(s_t).

PART D - DEBUGGING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
15. Episode returns flat at the random-policy level for 10k episodes.
    List four causes and one diagnostic each (reward scale, epsilon
    never decaying, LR too small/large, reward signal empty/broken).
16. DQN loss diverges to NaN. Which of the deadly triad (function
    approximation, bootstrapping, off-policy) is implicated and what
    two standard fixes do you apply first?
17. The agent finds a "solution" with high reward that violates the
    task (reward hacking): e.g. it parks in a corner farming points.
    How do you detect it, and which fix changes the objective vs the
    learning signal?
18. Learning curves look great in training but the deployed policy is
    useless. Name three causes (sim-to-real gap, eval distribution,
    reward misspecification) and the validation you should have run.
19. With a fixed seed the policy is great; across seeds, half the runs
    collapse. What do you report, and which algorithmic change most
    reduces seed variance (entropy bonus, clipping, larger replay)?

PART E - REAL WORLD / INTERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
20. RLHF pipeline: given a reward model r_phi, write the PPO objective
    for the LLM policy with the KL-to-reference penalty and say what
    each term prevents.
21. A self-driving lane-change planner is trained by RL in simulation.
    What breaks at deployment and what part of the stack (reward
    shaping, domain randomization, safety filter) addresses it?
22. (Interview) Explain the difference between supervised learning and
    RL using only the data available to each (i.i.d. labels vs
    non-stationary experience).
23. (Interview) "Why is RL hard to debug compared with supervised
    learning?" - answer with non-stationarity, credit assignment,
    exploration, and reward definition.
24. (Design) Recommend the RL-vs-heuristic-vs-supervised choice for:
    ad bidding, robot grasping, inventory restocking, chess. For each,
    say why RL is or is not the right tool.

PART F - EXPERT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
25. Implement clipped PPO on CartPole/gridworld (actor + critic +
    GAE if you can) and compare sample efficiency vs REINFORCE with
    baseline; report mean +/- std over 5 seeds.
26. (Open) Sparse-reward maze: implement reward shaping vs curiosity
    (prediction-error bonus) and measure which reaches the goal first
