CONCEPTUAL
 1. What impurity measure does CART use by default and why is it faster
    than entropy in practice?

    categories?

 4. Why is a single tree called a "weak learner"?
 5. What does the split point search do on a continuous feature?
 6. Why can't CART handle categorical features natively without encoding?
 7. What is the difference between a leaf predicting a class vs a
    probability?
 8. What is min_samples_split vs min_samples_leaf and which prevents
    tiny leaves more directly?

NUMERICAL
 9. A node has 10 positives / 10 negatives. Compute Gini impurity.

11. Split B: child1 (6 pos, 4 neg), child2 (4 pos, 6 neg). Compute
    weighted Gini. Which split is better, A or B?
12. Compute entropy of (8 pos, 2 neg). Now of (5, 5). Which is higher
    and what does that mean for the split?

DEBUGGING (find the bug)

20. The gain is computed against the FULL parent, not per-branch, so

21. Feature columns swapped between training and prediction produce
