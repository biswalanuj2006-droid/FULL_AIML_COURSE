# Module 24: Word Embeddings

Dense vector representations of words — the idea that unlocks modern NLP.

## What You Will Learn

- Why one-hot vectors fail: sparsity, no meaning
- Distributional hypothesis: "you shall know a word by its company"
- Word2Vec: CBOW and Skip-gram; negative sampling intuition
- GloVe and FastText: what each adds
- Properties: similarity, analogy (king − man + woman ≈ queen)
- Sentence/document embeddings and averaging limits
- Gensim usage; training your own embeddings
- Contextual embeddings preview (bridge to transformers)
